ProInfra — Civil 3D Infrastructure Tools Suite

A ribbon plugin suite for Autodesk Civil 3D covering gravity and pressure networks, profiles, QA/QC, labelling and data exchange.

NetMgr

Manage gravity pipe networks in one window: pipes, structures, trench sections, backdrops and BOQ.

PressMgr

Pressure network manager for pipes, fittings and appurtenances, with part-size swap and BOQ.

CrossNets

Detect and annotate network crossings in plan and profile, for gravity and pressure networks.

NetCheck

Read-only QA/QC of sewer networks: slopes, cover, manhole conditions and drafting standards.

MHFab

Manhole fabrication cards with ring and neck stacks, sections and precast summaries.

Tbl2Net

Build Civil 3D pipe networks from Excel, CSV or LandXML for round-trip exchange with hydraulic models.

SetSlope

Hover-driven editor for pipe inverts and slopes, directly in the drawing.

RenameSeq

Sequentially rename any Civil 3D object, with property filters and a live preview.

LabelDrag

Resolve overlapping labels automatically with shortest-leader placement.

Tbl2XL

Export AutoCAD and Civil 3D tables to Excel.
