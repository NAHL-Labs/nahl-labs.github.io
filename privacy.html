Privacy Policy

Applies to: ProInfra – Civil 3D Infrastructure Tools Suite and the ProInfra Tool Manager
Effective date: October 14, 2026

In short: ProInfra does not collect, store or sell any personal data. Your drawings and project data never leave your computer. The only network activity is the Tool Manager checking for and downloading updates from Dropbox.

1. Who we are

NAHL Labs is the publisher of ProInfra, a plugin suite for Autodesk Civil 3D. Contact: els.elsaadany@gmail.com.

2. Data the app collects

ProInfra does not collect personal data. Specifically, it does not:

  • require an account, login or registration;
  • collect names, email addresses or other identifying information;
  • include analytics, telemetry, tracking or advertising;
  • upload your drawings, models or project files anywhere.

All ProInfra commands run locally inside Civil 3D on your computer. Settings the tools save (for example window preferences) are stored only on your own computer.

3. Update checks (ProInfra Tool Manager)

The ProInfra Tool Manager connects to the internet to see whether a newer version is available and, when you choose to update, to download the new files. These files are hosted on Dropbox.

  • What is sent: a standard web request for the version file and update packages. No personal information, drawing data or usage data is included.
  • What Dropbox may receive: like any web server, Dropbox may automatically log technical details of the request, such as your IP address, the time of the request and basic software information. NAHL Labs does not receive or use this information.
  • Purpose: solely to deliver ProInfra updates.
